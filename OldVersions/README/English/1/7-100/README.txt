# Android x64

## About

<details open><summary><p><b>Click/tap here to expand/collapse the about section</b></p></summary>

Android x64 is a community project to virtualize and DeGoogle the Android operating system by Google, specifically the 64 bit versions.

See also: [:octocat: `Android x32`](https://github.com/seanpm2001/Android-x32/) and [:octocat: `Android x128`](https://github.com/seanpm2001/Android-x128/)

</details> <!-- End of About section !-->

## Builds

<details open><summary><p><b>Click/tap here to expand/collapse the builds section</b></p></summary>

Android x64 comes with 3 different builds for each Android release:

---

### Heavy

<details open><summary><p><b>Click/tap here to expand/collapse the heavy edition build section</b></p></summary>

- Heavy – Contains ALL of the Android libraries, and thus has to be re-licensed. Will run almost any app, similarly to Bluestacks
- License: ???

</details> <!-- End of Heavy section !-->

---

### Vanilla

<details open><summary><p><b>Click/tap here to expand/collapse the vanilla edition build section</b></p></summary>

- Vanilla – Middle option, bootable Android image with little modification.

</details> <!-- End of Vanilla section !-->

---

### DeGoogled

<details open><summary><p><b>Click/tap here to expand/collapse the DeGoogled edition build section</b></p></summary>

- DeGoogled – Removes all Google components, and phoning home methods.

---

#### Replacements

<details open><summary><p><b>Click/tap here to expand/collapse the replacements section</b></p></summary>

- Google Chrome -> DuckDuckGo web browser
- Google Play Store -> F-Droid
- Google Play Services -> MicroG (currently being decided on)
- YouTube (application) -> Unnamed (allows for any major video site to become a web application)
- Other (unlisted)
- Most Google programs -> Candroid

</details> <!-- End of Replacements section !-->

---

_End of DeGoogled build section._

</details> <!-- End of DeGoogled section !-->

---

#### Additional build

<details open><summary><p><b>Click/tap here to expand/collapse the additional build section</b></p></summary>

An additional build is available as a much larger file, it allows the choice of one of the 3 builds at installation, with Live CD support.

</details> <!-- End of Additional build section !-->

---

_End of Builds section._

</details> <!-- End of Builds section !-->

## Supported Android versions

<details open><summary><p><b>Click/tap here to expand/collapse the supported Android versions section</b></p></summary>

| <img src="/Android_Logo_HighDetail.png" alt="High-detail green Android robot failed to load" width="128" height="128" title="Android robot 3D"> |
|---|

Android 4.4 and below are x86 versions of Android, and are not covered by his project. All 64-bit Android versions (5.0 Lollipop and up, up to 16.0 (as of 2025, January 21st)) are planned to be supported. For versions 4.4 and below, please see [:octocat: `Android x32`](https://github.com/seanpm2001/Android-x32)
